Upload the exports the brand already has. RevenueOS derives customer behavior, ranks opportunities and resolves product recommendations from the connected catalog. Ambiguous columns can be mapped during onboarding; explicit opt-out is always suppressed.

Customers

Identity, consent and CRM attributes. Any delimiter and common European headers supported.

Optional if Transactions contain a customer ID/email. Missing consent is kept as Unknown; preview is allowed, outreach actions are blocked.
Transactions

Order history drives observed spend, AOV, frequency, recency and trajectory.

Needs a customer key and amount/revenue column; mappings are auto-detected.
